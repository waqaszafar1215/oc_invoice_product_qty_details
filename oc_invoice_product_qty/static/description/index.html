Quantity

See ordered and delivered quantities directly on every invoice line. No more switching between invoices, sale orders, and delivery orders to reconcile quantities.

Customer Invoices Vendor Bills Zero Config

WITHOUT THIS MODULE

Reconciling quantities is painful

Accountants must open the sale order, check delivered qty in the delivery order, then come back to compare with the invoice. This is time-consuming and error-prone, especially with partial deliveries.

WITH THIS MODULE

All quantities visible on the invoice

Ordered Qty and Delivered Qty appear as extra columns directly on invoice lines. Spot quantity mismatches instantly without leaving the invoice — and validate billing accuracy in seconds.

FEATURES

Quantity clarity on every invoice line
ORDERED QTY

Ordered Quantity Column

The originally ordered quantity from the sale order is displayed on each invoice line for easy reference.
DELIVERED QTY

Delivered Quantity Column

The actual delivered quantity from stock moves is shown alongside the invoiced quantity on each line.
VARIANCE

Instant Variance Visibility

Spot under-delivered or over-invoiced lines at a glance. Catch billing errors before they reach the customer.
BOTH TYPES

Customer Invoices & Vendor Bills

Works on outgoing customer invoices as well as incoming vendor bills linked to purchase orders.
PARTIAL

Partial Delivery Support

Correctly handles partial deliveries and multiple shipments — always reflects the true cumulative delivered quantity.
INSTANT

Zero Configuration

Install and the new columns appear automatically on all invoice lines. No settings page, no developer mode needed.
